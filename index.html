01 / About

Two disciplines,
one standard.

Most projects fall between a designer and a developer. I sit in both — so the idea survives the handoff.

5+ Years
Experience

I'm Amjad Ali, a graphic designer and full stack web developer focused on building modern, responsive and genuinely usable websites. I turn ideas into working digital experiences using clean, efficient and well-structured code.

My practice spans the whole line — branding and visual identity at one end, interface design and front-end development at the other — with WordPress and WooCommerce handling the content and commerce layer in between.

My goal is simple: websites that are visually impressive but also fast, responsive, accessible and easy to use. Beauty is the baseline, not the exception.
